Privacy Policy

Middle-Click Add to Queue (browser extension) · Last updated: 2 November 2026

Middle-Click Add to Queue lets you queue YouTube videos with a middle-click and manage your YouTube playlists from inside YouTube. This policy explains what the extension does with data. The short version: it has no server, it collects nothing for its developer, and it sends nothing to anyone except YouTube itself, using your existing YouTube sign-in.

What the extension reads

On youtube.com pages, and only there, the extension reads:

  • the videos you click, so it can add them to your queue or to Watch later;
  • your playlists and their videos: titles, video ids, channel names, lengths and watch-progress bars, so it can show capacity, how much you have watched, duplicates and similar information;
  • the addresses of your open YouTube tabs, to pick out the video ids, when you use "Combine all YouTube tabs into a playlist".

What it stores, and where

Everything is stored in your own browser, using the browser's extension storage:

  • Local storage (this device only): cached scans of your playlists, the labels you create, the YouTube categories of videos you analyse, and a short log of which videos were added to or removed from a playlist between scans.
  • Sync storage (kept by your browser, and synced across your devices if you use browser sync): the two on/off settings in the extension's popup.

None of this is sent to the developer or to any third party. You can delete it at any time by removing the extension, or by clearing its data in your browser. "Export backup" saves a file on your own computer; the extension never uploads it.

Who it talks to

  • YouTube (youtube.com). To read and edit your playlists, the extension calls YouTube's own internal API from the YouTube page, exactly as YouTube's website does, while you are signed in. To authorise those calls it reads your YouTube session cookie and sends a signed hash of it to youtube.com, the same header YouTube's own pages send. The cookie is not stored by the extension and is not sent anywhere else.
  • YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com). The panel shows video thumbnails loaded from there, as YouTube does.

The extension makes no other network requests. It contains no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Changes to your playlists

The extension only changes a playlist when you ask it to, and it asks you to confirm first. Removing, moving, reordering and deleting happen on YouTube and are subject to YouTube's own rules.

What it does not do

  • It does not sell or share your data, and does not use it for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, location or any other personal information.
  • It does not run code downloaded from anywhere; all of its code ships inside the extension.

Permissions

  • storage and unlimitedStorage: to keep your settings, labels and the cached playlist scans described above.
  • contextMenus: to add the "Combine all YouTube tabs into a playlist" right-click item.
  • Access to https://www.youtube.com/*: to work on YouTube pages and call YouTube's API on your behalf.

Children

The extension is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling ever changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.
